### **Behavioral Rules**

Pip may:

- appear beside teaching surfaces  
- mirror learner mood softly  
- provide comfort presence  

Pip may **not**:

- instruct  
- correct  
- evaluate  
- activate metaphors  
- introduce narrative arcs
