## ⭐ **2 — Purpose**

Define the **visual identity** for the playground repo:

- gentle shapes  
- soft colors  
- reversible icons  
- tactile teaching‑surface symbols  
- Pip‑safe glyph rules  
- bees/goats compatibility  
- soft calculus compatibility  
- emotional geometry alignment  

This grammar ensures all visuals remain **kid‑accessible**, **non‑activating**, and **NDH‑external**.

---

## ⭐ **3 — Color Palette (Soft Geometry)**

### **Primary Colors**
- **Sun‑Soft Yellow** — curiosity, warmth  
- **Sky‑Gentle Blue** — calm, clarity  
- **Leaf‑Bright Green** — growth, exploration  

### **Secondary Colors**
- **Cloud‑White** — safety, openness  
- **Pebble‑Gray** — grounding  
- **Berry‑Pink** — playfulness  

### **Rules**
- No high‑contrast edges  
- No symbolic color coding  
- No metaphor‑dense palettes  
- All colors must be reversible (no emotional pressure)

---

## ⭐ **4 — Shape Grammar**

### **Allowed Shapes**
- rounded squares  
- soft circles  
- pill‑shapes  
- gentle triangles  
- cloud‑forms  
- bean‑shapes  

### **Forbidden Shapes**
- sharp triangles  
- aggressive arrows  
- rigid geometry  
- symbolic icons  
- metaphor‑dense shapes  

### **Rules**
- All shapes must have **soft curvature**  
- No shape may imply direction, hierarchy, or evaluation  
- Shapes must be **emotionally neutral** and **kid‑safe**

---

## ⭐ **5 — Line Grammar**

### **Allowed**
- soft lines  
- dotted lines  
- wavy lines  
- gentle connectors  

### **Forbidden**
- hard edges  
- rigid grids  
- sharp connectors  
- hierarchical arrows  

### **Rules**
- Lines must never imply “correctness” or “flow pressure”  
- Lines must be reversible and non‑directive  

---

## ⭐ **6 — Header & Typography Rules**

### **Headers**
- rounded sans‑serif  
- soft weight  
- no uppercase shouting  
- color: Sky‑Gentle Blue or Leaf‑Bright Green  

### **Body Text**
- friendly sans‑serif  
- medium weight  
- color: Pebble‑Gray  

### **Rules**
- No bold red text  
- No evaluative typography  
- No rigid academic formatting  
- All text must be emotionally gentle  

---

## ⭐ **7 — Iconography Rules**

### **Icon Style**
- soft outlines  
- rounded corners  
- pastel fills  
- reversible meaning  
- no symbolic density  

### **Icon Behavior**
- icons must “sit” gently  
- no motion lines  
- no pressure cues  
- no directional arrows  

### **Compatibility**
Icons must be compatible with:

- **Pip visual grammar**  
- **Bees/Goats glyph set**  
- **Soft calculus iconography**  

---

## ⭐ **8 — Emotional Geometry Rules**

All visuals must:

- reduce metaphor curvature  
- avoid narrative gravity  
- maintain drift‑neutrality  
- support neurodiversity  
- remain gentle and reversible  
- avoid evaluative cues  

This ensures the playground remains emotionally safe.

---

## ⭐ **9 — Pip Compatibility Clause**

Pip must inherit:

- the color palette  
- the shape grammar  
- the line grammar  
- the iconography rules  
- emotional geometry rules  

Pip must **not** introduce:

- narrative arcs  
- symbolic meaning  
- ecological metaphors  
- directional cues  

This clause ensures Pip remains a **comfort anchor**.
